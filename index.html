Frame Photo Widget — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Frame Photo Widget is a free desktop photo frame for Windows, made by Michael Mu.

What the app does with your data

  • It collects nothing. It does not collect, store or send personal information, and it has no ads, no account and no analytics.
  • It makes no network connections. The app contains no network code. Your photos never leave your computer.
  • It reads your photos from the folders you choose (your Pictures folder by default) so it can show them. Nothing is uploaded or copied elsewhere.
  • It changes your files only when you click. Trash sends a photo to the Recycle Bin. Move puts it in a folder you pick. Both have an Undo. Nothing is changed on its own.
  • It keeps a small settings file on your computer in the app's own data folder: the photo folders you chose, folders to leave out, recently used Move folders, the time between photos and the window position. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
